prose-motions

A real Vim engine for Tiptap & ProseMirror — motions, operators, text-objects, registers, search, ex-commands and .-repeat, with per-editor isolation.

Both editors boot in Normal mode. Press i to insert, Esc to go back. The block caret sits on the character and inverts it — just like terminal Vim.

Tiptap NORMAL
ProseMirror NORMAL

Cheat sheet

Modes
i insert · a append · v visual · Esc normal
Open line
o below · O above
Motions
hjkl · w b e · 0 $ · gg G
Display lines
gj gk step wrapped rows
Operators
d delete · c change · y yank · p paste
Line ops
dd cc yy · 2dd with a count
Text objects
diw ciw daw ci"
Search
/text n N · *
Ex
:%s/a/b/g
Repeat / undo
. repeat · u undo · Ctrl-r redo
